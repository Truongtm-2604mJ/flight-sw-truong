LOTUS-1 flight software

Flight software for a small Earth observation satellite, on RTEMS 6 on a LEON3 processor (SIS) and operated from Yamcs. In the demonstration it rejects a forged command, detects a battery over-temperature, tries three bounded recoveries, goes to SAFE, and is recovered by an authenticated ground procedure.

What it does

  • TM/TC: CCSDS Space Packets, PUS-C services 1, 3, 5, 8, 11, 12, 17, 19, with verification reports for every command.
  • FDIR: limit monitors with persistence, heartbeat supervision with bounded restarts, a watchdog, bounded thermal recovery.
  • Modes: NOMINAL / DEGRADED / SAFE, generated from a model and checked against it in CI. SAFE is left only by ground command.
  • Security: HMAC-SHA-256 authentication with anti-replay, key rotation, a minimal recovery key, no reply to forged commands.
